Runtime does not download data or call a paid provider. Country polygons are
generalised geographical references, not an official boundary survey. Points
within2km of another country are not assigned a guessed country. At a crossing,
choose the explicitly verified catalogue object and side of border. The catalogue
also records source URLs and mapped checkpoint coordinates; those coordinates
are not a promise that a particular vehicle may use a traffic lane.
